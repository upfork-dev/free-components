# Photographs and images

**The demo holds 10 pairs: 5 are real photographs of physical shoes, and 5 are AI-generated images.** We made the AI images on 2026-11-05, with Grok and ChatGPT, because we could not find enough real, logo-free side-view photographs. Nothing here says that all of them are real.

None of these pictures is covered by this product's MIT licence. Each keeps its own licence or terms, below. A shop should replace them with its own.

## The 5 real photographs

No AI upscaling or inpainting was used on these. Removing the background, mirroring, levelling, exposure, and cloning a small mark from the same panel are the only edits.
